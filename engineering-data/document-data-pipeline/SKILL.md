# Document a data pipeline

Record:

- purpose and owner;
- source systems and input contracts;
- important transformations and business rules;
- destinations and downstream consumers;
- schedule, latency, and retention;
- monitoring and data-quality checks; and
- failure recovery and backfill procedure.

Identify sensitive data and access constraints. Link to existing schemas or dashboards instead of duplicating volatile details.
