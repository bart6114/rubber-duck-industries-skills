# Prepare an onboarding plan

Organize the plan into:

- **Before day one:** access, equipment, schedule, and named buddy.
- **First day:** welcome, role context, essential setup, and key introductions.
- **First week:** systems, team practices, initial learning, and a small first task.
- **Days 8–30:** role-specific goals, feedback moments, and a clear first deliverable.

Assign an owner to each setup task. Mark missing details as questions rather than making up company policy.
