# Write a runbook

Include:

1. purpose and scope;
2. prerequisites and required access;
3. numbered steps with expected results;
4. verification that the procedure succeeded;
5. rollback or safe-stop instructions; and
6. escalation owner and conditions.

Call out destructive or irreversible steps before they occur. Do not assume credentials, permissions, or production access.
