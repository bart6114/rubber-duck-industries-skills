# Draft an incident update

State:

- current status;
- confirmed impact and affected users or systems;
- what responders are doing now;
- workarounds, if verified; and
- when the next update will be provided.

Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses. Do not assign blame, estimate recovery without evidence, or expose sensitive operational details.
