# Draft a company announcement

Write a short title followed by:

- what is changing;
- why it matters;
- who is affected;
- what readers need to do; and
- the effective date or next update, when known.

Lead with the change, use plain language, and keep background secondary. Do not invent dates, policy details, or approvals.
