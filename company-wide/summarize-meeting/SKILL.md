# Summarize a meeting

Produce these sections:

1. **Summary** — the purpose and outcome in a short paragraph.
2. **Decisions** — decisions that were actually made.
3. **Actions** — action, owner, and deadline when stated.
4. **Open questions** — unresolved points that need follow-up.

Do not turn suggestions into decisions. Mark missing owners or deadlines as `Unassigned` or `Not set` rather than inventing them.
